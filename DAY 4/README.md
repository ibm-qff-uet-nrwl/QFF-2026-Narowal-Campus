### 📚 Topics Covered

- **Circuit Depth:** Gates take time, why depth matters, and comparing circuits with the same result but different depths.
- **Reducing Depth:** GHZ state preparation, fan-out vs. chain, and recursive fan-out methods.
- **Transpilation:** Hardware constraints, coupling maps, steps of the Qiskit transpiler, and how transpilation can increase depth.
- **Heavy-Hex Topology:** How Heron qubits connect and building GHZ states on heavy-hex architecture.
