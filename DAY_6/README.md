### 📚 Topics Covered / احاطہ کیے گئے موضوعات
Classical Registers: Grouping bits with a name, size, and order to store measurement outcomes.

Mid-Circuit Measurement: Measuring a qubit in the middle of the circuit without collapsing the entire computation.

Conditional Logic & Feedforward: Using if_test to turn measurement outcomes into active choices and branching circuit execution.

Dynamic Circuits: Building a constant-depth GHZ state using measurement, reset, and feedforward.
